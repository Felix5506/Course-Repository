Top 5 Animes

My personal top 5 from what I have seen

  1. Steins;gate

    An anime about time travel

  2. Jojos Bizarre Adventure

    Including all parts and Steel Ball Run so far. I have not yet finished the manga

  3. Death Note

    Death Note was the first anime I watched so I consider it in my top 3 often.

  4. 91 Days

    Essentially its an anime where the protagonist tries to take revenge on a mafia by joining it and taking it down from the inside

  5. Re:Zero

    I am a fan of animes with some form of time travel, I am not yet caught up on Re:Zero but it is good enough to be in my top 5 so far
